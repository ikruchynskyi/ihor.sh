    What the labels and flags mean
    • ✓ at the employer: from the employer's own careers site or the government's own listings (NYC and USAJOBS). A job taken down there drops off here.
    • via Adzuna: a job board aggregator; its listings stay 30 days from their posting date. Gig ads and reposts are left out.
    • Flags never hide a job unless you ask: open N days (possibly a ghost job), evergreen / talent-pool (collecting résumés, not hiring for a seat), no salary range (NYC's pay-transparency law requires one for jobs here), staffing agency, commission-only or agent-recruiting, very wide pay range, scam signs. Also: posted again N times in 6 months (taken down and put up again: a ghost job, or a role nobody stays in), entry-level title asking for N+ years, wording that screens people out (age-coded, native-speaker, "rockstar"), always-on culture wording. In a job's details, 🔍 Check this job has AI read the posting against this site's record of the employer: every flag cites that record or quotes the posting, with a question to ask the recruiter.
    • Salaries are what the employer posted; Adzuna's estimates are marked "est." and don't count as stated pay.
