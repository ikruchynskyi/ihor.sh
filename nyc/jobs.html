    How this works, and what the flags mean
    • ✓ at the employer: read from the company's own job board (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby) or NYC's official listings. These are checked every hour; a job missing twice in a row is removed.
    • via Adzuna: a job board aggregator. We can't open their pages to check, so these stay while Adzuna still lists them and go a week after it stops. Gig ads (delivery apps, "be your own boss") and reposts (the same ad again, or copied to many towns) never get in.
    • Flags never hide a job unless you ask: open N days (possibly a ghost job), evergreen / talent-pool (collecting résumés, not hiring for a seat), no salary range (NYC's pay-transparency law requires one for jobs here), staffing agency, commission-only or agent-recruiting, very wide pay range, scam signs.
    • Salaries are what the employer posted; Adzuna's estimates are marked "est." and don't count as stated pay.
