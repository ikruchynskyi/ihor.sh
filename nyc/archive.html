How this works

A lightweight server worker queries the MTA's real-time feeds every 5 minutes: subway alerts and elevator and escalator outages. Outages are recorded with their start and resolution times, giving accurate downtime without spamming queries.

Data: MTA GTFS-RT. Independent open-source archive, not affiliated with the MTA.
