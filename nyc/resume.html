How a hiring system reads your résumé

Most applications are read by software (an applicant tracking system) before a person sees them. It pulls out plain text, guesses your name, contact details, jobs and skills, and often filters on the exact words a posting uses. This page shows you that plain text, finds the layout choices that break it, checks the basics, and, given a job, lists the terms you're missing. It never invents anything for you to claim.

🔒 Your file is read here in your browser. Nothing is uploaded; nothing is kept unless you tick "keep it in this browser".
