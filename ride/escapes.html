Rail Rules & Best Practices

  • Platform courtesy: Walk your bike on platforms and through stations; never ride inside terminals. Always yield doors and priority spaces to riders with disabilities.
  • Electric bikes: E-bikes and scooters are strictly prohibited on NJ Transit trains. Check MTA guidelines before carrying lithium battery bikes on Metro-North or LIRR.
  • Conductor authority: If a train car is at maximum capacity or a crew member issues an instruction, train crew discretion supersedes standard timetables.
