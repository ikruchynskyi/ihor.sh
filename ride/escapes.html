Bike + train escapes

Leave the city on a train with your bike, ride to a campground, come back the next day. The hard part isn't the riding: it's knowing which trains take a full-size bike. Each railroad bans bikes at rush hour and on certain holidays, with different hours, so the checker below applies all of their rules for you. Every rule comes from the railroad's own page (linked), checked October 10, 2026.

Make your own escape

Pick a station and how far you'd ride, and the page lists every campground mapped in OpenStreetMap around it. Measure the real bike ride to any of them, open it in the route notebook, and save the trip to your list. These come straight from the map, so they aren't checked the way the trips above are: look at the campground's own website for its season and how to book, and whether it takes tents for one night.

Good to know

  • Walk your bike in stations and on platforms; bikes go where they don't block aisles or doors, and riders with disabilities always come first.
  • E-bikes and scooters aren't allowed on NJ Transit trains; check the MTA's rules for electric bikes before you bring one on Metro-North or the LIRR.
  • Rules change. The checker follows the pages below as of the date above; if a conductor or a sign says otherwise, they win.
