Gear list

Weigh what you carry, and know what it can do: how much water your bottles hold, how many days your batteries last against what your phone and lights use, how many days of food are in the bags. The route notebook reads these to warn about dry stretches and flat batteries. Everything stays in this browser.

Battery watt-hours: a power bank's mAh × 3.7 V ÷ 1000 (a "10,000 mAh" bank is 37 Wh; expect about 80% of it after conversion losses, which the totals take off). What devices use a day: a phone in airplane mode with GPS around 5–8 Wh, a bike light on medium 2–4 Wh an hour. "Worn" items don't count toward what the bike carries.
