Learn & build

The learning paths behind everything else on this site, published as I go. Every lesson is used somewhere real: filters in the radio, statistics on the NYC archives, a driver for my own USB dongle. Same approach as the other worlds: picture first, then the math, and everything live.

⚡ Electronics from scratch

Circuits you can tune, solved live by a small circuit simulator written for this course.

  1. Voltage, current, resistance

    Ohm's law with current you can watch, power and heat, voltage dividers and reading a light sensor, parallel branches, and the resistor every LED needs.

  2. Capacitors and time

    Charging curves, the RC time constant, and the simplest filter: the same one the radio course uses.

    Used in: the radio's filters, debouncing buttons, power supply smoothing.

  3. Coils, resonance and antennas

    Inductors, LC tanks ringing at one frequency, and why an antenna's length matters.

    Used in: tuning the RTL-SDR, Meshtastic antennas.

  4. Diodes and transistors

    One-way valves, and a MOSFET as a switch a microcontroller can flip, with the flyback diode it needs.

  5. Op-amps

    Amplifiers built from feedback: buffers, gain, and active filters.

  6. From gates to a computer

    Logic gates you can click, an adder, a flip-flop, and how they become a CPU.

  7. Talking to chips: UART, I²C, SPI

    Waveforms from a logic analyzer, decoded bit by bit, then a driver written from a datasheet.

  8. Power: batteries and regulators

    Lithium cells and their protection, LDO vs buck, and an efficiency budget for a battery project.

  9. Designing a PCB

    From schematic to KiCad layout to a board in the mail.
