Circuit lab

Build your own circuits and watch them work. Pick a part, click where it starts and where it ends on the grid; wire things together; the yellow dots show the current, solved live. Try burning out an LED, shorting a battery, or charging a capacitor through a switch.

How to build

1. Pick a part above. 2. Click a grid point where it starts, then where it ends (parts are 3 squares long; wires any length). 3. Join everything with wires into a loop through a battery.

Select a part to change its value. R rotates it, Delete removes it. Click a switch to flip it. Hover anywhere to read the voltage there.

Solved by the same simulator as the electronics chapters: modified nodal analysis with diodes and capacitors stepped through time. Batteries have 0.1 Ω inside them, like real ones. Your circuit is saved in this browser.
