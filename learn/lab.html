Circuit lab

Build a circuit, watch the current flow, and put a scope on it. Pick a part and click to drop it (R turns it), wire pins together, or insert a ready-made circuit. Everything is simulated live by the same solver as the electronics chapters.

Oscilloscope Drop a scope probe (Meters) on any point to see its voltage over time.

Models: Shockley diodes and LEDs (they burn out above 30 mA), square-law MOSFETs, op-amps with gain 100,000 that saturate 1.5 V from their rails, a behavioral 555 (5 kΩ divider, two comparators, a flip-flop), a 78xx regulator with 2 V dropout, 5 V CMOS logic. Batteries have 0.1 Ω inside, sources 1 Ω. Your circuit is saved in this browser.

Insert a circuit

It's added to your board where you click next (Esc cancels). Probes come with it, so the scope shows it straight away.
