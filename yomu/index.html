World 4 · 読む yomu = to read

Yomu: Japanese from zero

Learn by reading little stories about Yuki, Sam and Blip in New York. Every word can be tapped, every line can be heard and said back. Behind the stories sits the whole JLPT N5–N3 syllabus (kana, grammar, kanji and words), kanji and word decks all the way to N1, spoken scenes and handwriting, with spaced review so nothing slips away.
