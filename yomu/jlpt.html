JLPT N5–N1 Exam Trainer

Realistic mock exam simulation for the Japanese Language Proficiency Test. Features timed sections, grammar star sentences (文の組み立て), authentic cloze, reading passages, listening tasks, and official sectional threshold scoring (180 points total).

⚠️ Unofficial Practice Simulator & Diagnostic Trainer

This trainer is an independent educational tool designed to simulate the pacing, question formats, and scoring mechanics of the JLPT. It is NOT an official JLPT test and does NOT confer official certification or formal scores.

Official registration, worldwide exam dates (typically held in July and December), test site locators, and official certification can only be acquired through the official sponsors: The Japan Foundation and Japan Educational Exchanges and Services (JEES).
