2. Drill

Pick a drill. It uses the rows you ticked (or the first three rows).

3. Tips

  • Say each sound out loud as you see it; your ear and eye learn together.
  • Write each new character five times on paper, following the stroke order. Hand memory is strong.
  • Small っ doubles the next consonant (きって kitte), ー stretches a vowel in katakana (コーヒー koohii), and small ゃゅょ merge with the sound before (きゃ kya).
  • は, へ and を are read wa, e and o when they're particles.
