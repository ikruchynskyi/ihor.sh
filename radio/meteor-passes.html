Recorded Meteor passes

This site's radio in New York records every Meteor-M2 3 and M2 4 pass that spends 1 minute or more in the north sky between 20° and 60° up by itself (the only part of the sky its antenna on a north-facing balcony hears): a minute before the satellite rises, the server takes its dongle (nobody else can use it for those 15 minutes), records the signal, and decodes it on the server. Each pass gets a report card below: the five stages the signal goes through, how they went over time, and the pictures. Open one in the picture maker for color, maps and coastlines. Passes with a usable picture are kept indefinitely; all passes are kept for the last 3 days. Two raw recordings are kept, for opening in SatDump: the newest pass whose picture shows the Earth (more than half its pixels neither black nor white, at least a minute of picture), and the newest one whose picture doesn't, for checking what went wrong.
