Radio from scratch

A visual course that builds a software radio receiver from the ground up, the way 3Blue1Brown teaches math: every idea starts as something you watch move, and formulas come last, as short labels for what you already understand. Along the way you'll build, line by line, the receiver that runs on this site, and from chapter 3 on, try each idea on real radio.

You need school math and a little curiosity. Each chapter lists what it builds on, with links back.

Also: Spectrum Lab (the full app) · one-page quick reference · build for your own dongle (developer guide).
