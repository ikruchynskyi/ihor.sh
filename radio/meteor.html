Meteor pass recorder

Russia's Meteor-M satellites send weather pictures as LRPT: digital QPSK, 72,000 symbols a second, about 120 kHz wide. Start the dongle a minute before the pass, press record, and stop when it sets. The page decodes the picture live as the signal comes in (Doppler is tracked automatically, no retuning), and the IQ file lets you decode it again later. Pass times are just below; how LRPT works is chapter 21.

    Turning it into a picture

    • This page decodes in your browser (no Reed-Solomon error correction yet, so damaged packets show as streaks). For the best result, open the file in SatDump: Offline processing → METEOR M2-x LRPT 72k, baseband format cu8, sample rate from the file name. It demodulates, Viterbi-decodes, de-randomizes and decodes Meteor's JPEG into the visible and infrared channels.
    • Antenna: a V-dipole (two 53 cm rods at 120°, horizontal, outdoors) beats anything on a windowsill. Passes under 20° rarely decode.
    • Files are about 30 MB a minute and live only in this tab (nothing is uploaded): a recording stops itself at 20 minutes, and the tab keeps the last 3. Download the ones you want to keep.
