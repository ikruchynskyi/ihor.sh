CONNECTING… ·

Airliners broadcast who and where they are twice a second on 1090 MHz. A $30 dongle on a Mac in New York hears them, and our own decoder (no dump1090, written from scratch) turns the pulses into this map.

The receiver is off. One dongle is shared with Spectrum Lab and the APRS map.
