A visual companion to the ARRL Handbook

The ARRL Handbook is the classic reference for radio electronics: a thousand pages from Ohm's law to antenna arrays. It's thorough, but a printed page can't move. This companion follows the Handbook chapter by chapter and section by section, and points each one to an interactive explanation on this site: the license lessons, the Radio from scratch course, and labs written for topics that needed something new. Read a section, then come here and play with it.

Organized by the table of contents of the 100th edition (section titles only, for orientation). All explanations here are this site's own. Sections without a link (references, projects, some construction topics) are best read in the book itself.
