What is this?

Meshtastic turns cheap LoRa radios (915 MHz in the US, under a watt) into a text-messaging mesh with no phones, internet or towers. Each radio repeats what it hears, up to a few hops, so messages travel across the city from rooftop to rooftop. Our node sits on a desk on USB; this page shows every node it has heard and the public channel's chat.

Under the hood: the node speaks protobuf over serial, framed by the bytes 0x94 0xC3 and a length. Our server decodes it with a few dozen lines of our own TypeScript (no Meshtastic library). SNR is how far above the noise a signal arrived (LoRa still decodes below 0 dB, down to about −20); hops is how many radios relayed it. Positions are as nodes broadcast them, usually rounded on purpose.
