WORLD 1RADIO

ON AIR

A software-defined radio that runs in your browser, written from scratch in TypeScript with no SDR libraries: just math and a cheap USB dongle. Plus courses that teach every piece of it, and prep for the ham radio license.

1-1 ▶ PLAYSPECTRUM LABA receiver you can watch stage by stage: waterfall, mixer, filter, demodulation, audio. 1-2 ▶ PLAYHOW A SOFTWARE RADIO WORKSThe whole receiver in plain words, one stage at a time. 1-3 ▶ PLAYRADIO FROM SCRATCH16 interactive chapters, from a dot on a wheel to a USB driver. 1-4 ▶ PLAYHAM LICENSE PREPLessons and practice exams for the US amateur radio license. 1-5 ▶ PLAYBUILD FOR YOUR DONGLEA developer guide to WebUSB, the RTL2832U and the R820T tuner. 1-6 ▶ PLAYHANDBOOK COMPANIONThe ARRL Handbook chapter by chapter, each section linked to an interactive lesson. 1-7 ▶ PLAYAPRS MAPLive packet radio around NYC, decoded by Direwolf. CHECKING… 1-8 ▶ PLAYADS-B RADARAircraft over NYC, decoded from 1090 MHz by dump1090. CHECKING… 1-9 ▶ PLAYSSTV DECODERSlow-scan TV pictures from the ISS, a recording, your mic or the dongle.
